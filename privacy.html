Weir — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 Last updated: 28 October 2026 (Smart insights section, Weir 2.0) Developer: Novemade (Burak Yılmaz), İstanbul, Türkiye Contact: info@novemade.com

Weir is a digital-wellbeing app for iPhone and Android that lets you keep Instagram and YouTube while turning off the surfaces you choose (Reels, Explore, Shorts, the Home feed). It is built so that it cannot learn much about you. This policy explains exactly what the app touches, what it stores, and what it never does.

1. No account, no servers

Weir has no sign-up and no login. We do not operate a backend for the app. Your rules, schedules, allowances, streaks, statistics and (from Weir 2.0) smart insights are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase everything from Settings → Erase everything, or export a copy as a JSON file from Settings → Export my data.

2. What the app reads on your device, and why

Android — Accessibility service

With your explicit consent in the app, Weir registers an Android accessibility service bound only to the Instagram and YouTube apps. The service receives window-change and tap events from those two apps and inspects the on-screen layout: the identifiers of its elements, their size and visibility, and the accessibility labels of navigation elements such as the bottom tabs (for example “Reels”, “Shorts” or “Subscriptions”), including the label of a tab you tap. It compares these against a fixed list to decide whether a surface you turned off (for example the Reels viewer or the Shorts player) is visible.

When it is, Weir takes one of three actions on your behalf: it sends the system Back action and shows its own intention screen; for YouTube’s Home feed, it taps YouTube’s Subscriptions tab instead, so you stay in the app without the feed; and when you have paused Instagram or YouTube, or used up its daily allowance, it sends the system Home action and shows its intention screen. It performs no other actions in those apps.

The service does not read, store or transmit message text, comments, captions, usernames, passwords, images or any other content. It records only: that a surface was blocked, which surface, and when; and how many seconds a surface or app was in the foreground. These counters stay on the device.

The accessibility service is not an accessibility tool in the sense of Google Play's policy; it is used for the app's core feature, as disclosed in the app before you enable it.

Android — Usage access (optional)

If you grant Usage access, Weir reads the system's foreground-time statistics for Instagram and YouTube only, to show how long you used each app today. No other apps are queried.

iPhone — Screen Time (Family Controls)

With your authorisation, Weir uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) to pause the apps you pick during your schedules and after your daily allowance. Apple gives Weir only opaque tokens for the apps you choose; Weir never learns which apps they are, and Apple does not share your usage with us. Shield screens and threshold events are handled in app extensions on your device.

iPhone — Safari extension and content blocker

Weir's Safari web extension runs only on instagram.com and youtube.com. It reads the current page address, and the address of a link you tap, to hide or intercept Reels, Explore, Shorts and the Home feed; while it covers one of those surfaces it pauses the video playing underneath. It notes that a surface was blocked. It does not read page content, forms or cookies and does not communicate with any server. The content blocker contains fixed URL rules for the same two sites.

Smart insights (Weir 2.0 and later) This section applies from Weir 2.0, the version that introduces Smart insights. Earlier versions do not have the feature.

What it uses. Weir works out your week, your patterns and personalised suggestions on your device from records the app already keeps: the blocked attempts it counted (which surface, when), the days protection was on, your rules and schedules and, on Android, the time measured on turned-off screens and in Instagram and YouTube (see Usage access above). On iPhone, Apple does not share app usage with Weir, so time in apps is not measured there. Weir also stores, on the device, which suggestions you dismissed or applied and the weekly goal you set.

Suggestions. A suggestion (for example a schedule, a pause, a daily allowance or a weekly goal) changes your protection only when you tap Apply. Suggestions never loosen protection.

On-device language model (iPhone only). On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on and in a supported language, Weir can ask the phone's built-in on-device language model (Apple's Foundation Models framework) to word your weekly summary. It receives only a few aggregate numbers that Weir already shows on the screen (for example the number of attempts this week and last week). The model runs on your phone; nothing is sent to Weir, Apple or anyone else. If the model is not available, or its answer does not match Weir's numbers, Weir shows its own fixed sentence instead.

Android. Weir uses no language model on Android. The summary and suggestions are produced by fixed rules in the app.

No remote AI, no sharing. Weir does not send your usage, statistics or insights to any server, AI service or third party. Smart insights are never combined with advertising data and are not used to choose or personalise ads.

Your choices. In Settings → Smart insights you can turn off Insights and suggestions (no analysis is made), turn off On-device summary (the model is never asked) and Reset suggestions and goal. Settings → Erase everything removes all of this together with your other data; deleting the app does the same.

3. Advertising (free version only)

Free users see small banner ads from Google AdMob on the Today and Statistics screens only. Ads never appear during onboarding, permissions, protection activation, interventions, purchases or privacy screens. Google may process device identifiers and approximate location to serve ads, as described in Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by law, Weir shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before loading ads, and you can change your choice in that form.

Premium users see no ads, and the advertising SDK is never initialised in a Premium session.

4. Purchases

Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by the store; Weir never sees your payment details. The app keeps a local record of your entitlement so Premium keeps working offline. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your store account settings.

5. Analytics and tracking

Weir contains no third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. A small set of first-use milestones (for example "protection enabled for the first time") is counted locally on your device and is never uploaded.

6. Children

Weir is intended for adults aged 18 and over; its target audience on Google Play is set to 18+. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Because Weir does not hold personal data about you on any server, requests to access, correct or delete data are fulfilled directly on your device through the app's Export and Erase functions. For any question or request, write to info@novemade.com.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when the policy changes. Material changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
